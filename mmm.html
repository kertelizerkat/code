Question 1.1 (Base Campaign Model)

(a) Objective Function: Maximize $Z = 8000x + 15000y$ (Maximisation problem to maximize total campaign audience reach).

(b) Constraints:
• Budget: $1500x + 4000y \le 30000 \implies 3x + 8y \le 60$
• Labor hours: $2.25x + 5y \ge 28$ (2h 15m is $2.25$ hours)
• Brand presence: $x \ge y \implies x - y \ge 0$
• Local Station limit: $y \le 5$
• Client Spam cap: $x \le 12$
• Realities: $x, y \ge 0$

(e) Binding Constraints: Budget limit ($3x+8y \le 60$) and Client Spam cap ($x \le 12$).

(f) Optimal Allocation: 12 Social Media Posts and 3 Radio Ads.

(g) Optimal Audience Reach: 141,000 people ($8000(12) + 15000(3)$).

Question 1.2 (Social Reach drops to 5625)

(a) Iso-Reach Path: With objective $Z = 5625x + 15000y$, the isoreach slope ($-\frac{5625}{15000} = -0.375$) is exactly parallel to the budget slope ($-\frac{1500}{4000} = -0.375$).

Evaluating corner nodes:

  • Point $(5, 5)$: $5625(5) + 15000(5) = 103,125$
  • Point $(6.67, 5)$: $5625(6.67) + 15000(5) = \mathbf{112,500}$ (Optimal)
  • Point $(12, 3)$: $5625(12) + 15000(3) = \mathbf{112,500}$ (Optimal)
  • Point $(12, 0.2)$: $5625(12) + 15000(0.2) = 70,500$

Because the objective slope matches the budget limit, there are **infinite optimal solutions** along the boundary segment connecting **$(6.67, 5)$** and **$(12, 3)$** where the optimal isoreach line equations overlaps the budget boundary.

(b) Optimal Reach: 112,500 people.

Question 1.3 (Social Reach surges to 21000)

(a) Iso-Reach Path: Objective shifts to $Z = 21000x + 15000y$, with a steep slope of $m = -\frac{21}{15} = -1.4$.

Evaluating corner nodes:

  • Point $(5, 5)$: $21000(5) + 15000(5) = 180,000$
  • Point $(6.67, 5)$: $21000(6.67) + 15000(5) = 215,000$
  • Point $(12, 3)$: $21000(12) + 15000(3) = \mathbf{297,000}$ (Optimal)
  • Point $(12, 0.2)$: $21000(12) + 15000(0.2) = 255,000$

The optimal line equation is $21000x + 15000y = 297000$ (with intercepts at $(14.14,0)$ and $(0, 19.8)$), touching the feasible region corner at point $(12, 3)$ which remains the absolute maximum.

(b) Optimal Reach: 297,000 people.

Question 1.4 (Slope Rules Comparison)
"As the relative audience reach of social media posts increases across Q1.2 ($m=-0.375$), Q1.1 ($m=-0.533$), and Q1.3 ($m=-1.4$), the slope of the iso-reach line becomes increasingly steep, pivoting the optimal campaign configuration along the budget boundary from an entire parallel line segment (Q1.2) to a specific corner solution locked at the maximum social posts limit (Q1.1 & Q1.3)."
